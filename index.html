Model assumptions
  • Deterministic engine, integer minutes, closures are [start, end).
  • Flights process in scheduled-departure order; delay is absorbed by turnaround buffer above 30 min.
  • A flight cannot depart or land inside a closure; it is ground-held at origin.
  • Crew timeout (arrival minus duty start above max) cancels that flight and all later flights of the crew. No standby crews.
  • Stranded = pax on cancelled flights + pax of missed connections (no double counting).
  • Capacity mode, recovery actions and AI dispatcher are not in this build.
